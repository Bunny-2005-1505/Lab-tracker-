ℹ️ About Lab Tracker

Lab Tracker is a simple digital inventory for school and college labs. It replaces paper registers so nothing gets lost, runs out, or expires unnoticed.

  • Track chemicals and equipment by lab, quantity and expiry date.
  • Scan a QR code when adding a product, or type the code yourself.
  • Get reminders by email 5 days and 1 day before something expires.
  • Lab Assistants manage the products they add. Verified Teachers can see all products in their college.
  • Teachers are verified with their college email and a photo of their ID card. The ID is checked by AI, reviewed by the administrator if anything looks wrong, never shown to other users, and deleted once the decision is made.
