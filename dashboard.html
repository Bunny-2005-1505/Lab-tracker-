    About Lab Tracker

    Lab Tracker keeps a lab's chemicals and equipment in one list, with quantities and expiry dates, so stock is easy to check and nothing expires unnoticed.

    Adding products
    Type a product's code, or scan its QR code with your phone camera.
    Expiry reminders
    You get an email 5 days and 1 day before a product expires.
