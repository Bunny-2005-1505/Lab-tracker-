What Lab Tracker does

One-tap usage log

Tap "Used 1" next to any item. Stock updates instantly, and a mis-tap can be undone within 10 minutes.

Stock-out warnings

Lab Tracker learns how fast each item is used and shows when it will run out, so you can reorder in time.

Expiry reminder emails

Automatic emails 5 days and 1 day before a product expires, so nothing is wasted.

Equipment booking

Teachers reserve instruments for practical sessions. Overlapping bookings are blocked automatically.

Maintenance and returns

Mark equipment as under maintenance, and see overdue returns at a glance on your dashboard.

Many labs

CSE Lab, Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, or add your own lab name. Add items by typing or scanning a QR code.

Organization access

Staff join with a college or company email. Verified lab teachers see their organization's stock and equipment; assistants see their own.

Secure login

Sign in with email or Google, and turn on two-step verification (MFA) for extra protection.

Works on phone and laptop

A clean layout for small and large screens, plus a built-in chat assistant for help.

How it works

  1. Register with your college or company email and confirm it.
  2. Add your lab products with quantity, unit, expiry date and a reorder level.
  3. Tap "Used 1" whenever something is used. It takes one second.
  4. Get warned before items run out or expire, by dashboard alerts and email.
  5. Book equipment for practical sessions. Lab teachers can also block time for maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lab Tracker?

Lab Tracker is a web-based inventory management system for laboratories. It helps labs keep track of chemicals, instruments and consumables and avoid using expired items.

Is Lab Tracker free?

Yes, you can register and use it for free.

Who can see my products?

Lab assistants see only their own products. Verified lab teachers can also see the products of people from the same organization. Different organizations cannot see each other's data.

How do I become a lab teacher?

Log in with your official college or company email, open the dashboard and choose Verify as Lab Teacher. You will be asked for your designation and a photo of your staff ID card.

How does the stock-out warning work?

Each time you tap "Used 1", Lab Tracker records it. From the last 14 days of usage it estimates how many days are left. Items are marked Critical if they are out of stock, at their reorder level, or will run out within 7 days, and Watch if they will run out within 21 days. If an item has not been logged for 14 days, it is flagged because the estimate may be wrong.

How does equipment booking work?

Open Equipment, tap a free time slot, choose your times and confirm. If someone already booked that instrument, or it is under maintenance, the booking is refused and you are shown when it is free. Bookings that end without being marked returned appear as overdue returns.

Who can add equipment or schedule maintenance?

Verified lab teachers can add equipment, schedule maintenance and export booking data. Lab assistants can book equipment and manage their own bookings.

How do expiry reminders work?

Every morning the system checks for products expiring in 5 days or 1 day and emails the person who added them.
